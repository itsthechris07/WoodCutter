Made for survival servers

Everything is a setting: who cuts trees, how big they are and what it costs.

Every tree

Oaks, jungle giants, cherry trees and the huge fungi of the Nether – also branches with gaps like those of acacias.

Falling animation

The tree falls log by log from the bottom to the top, blocks-per-tick sets the speed. Or all at once.

Buildings stay

Logs that were placed by players are never cut, so the log cabin next to the tree is safe.

Fast leaf decay

The leaves of a cut tree decay within seconds instead of minutes. Placed leaves stay.

Replanting

A new sapling is planted where the tree stood – for free or taken from the inventory of the player.

Drops

Logs go straight into the inventory if you like, with a chance that a log drops a second time.

Delay & costs

Seconds between two trees, food points or experience levels per tree. Ranks can skip the delay.

Plays well with others

Every log is broken as the player: protection, logging and skill plugins see each one of them.

Worlds

Switch WoodCutter off in single worlds, by name or with a pattern: sg_* covers every arena.

Commands

/woodcutter toggle for players, enchant and give for admins – or as a reward from the console.

Paper & Folia

One jar for Paper 1.21 to 26.2 and for Folia. No other plugin is needed, not even for permissions.

Your texts

Every message is in messages.yml, with colors and your prefix. Empty texts are not sent.
