One log, whole tree

WoodCutter is an enchantment for axes – from the enchanting table, an anvil or a command.

CUT A TREE

an axe · one log · the whole tree
1
Get the enchantment
Enchant an axe or a book for 30 levels: with a chance of 40 % it gets WoodCutter as well.
2
Sneak and break a log
Break a log with the enchanted axe while sneaking. Without sneaking it is a single log, as always.
3
The tree falls log by log
From the bottom to the top, as if you had broken every log yourself: the axe takes damage, the logs drop.
4
Leaves decay, a sapling grows
The leaves are gone within seconds – and a new sapling is planted in place of the tree, if you like.
Only the logs above the broken one fall – break the lowest log to cut the whole tree.
