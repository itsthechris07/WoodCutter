# WoodCutter 3.0.0

![WoodCutter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/itsthechris07/WoodCutter/main/branding/banner.png)

WoodCutter is back: a rewrite for current Minecraft versions, with levels for the enchantment and a lot of new
settings. The last release (2.0.7) was made for Minecraft 1.14.

**Requirements:** Paper or Folia 1.21 or newer (tested up to 26.2), Java 21. Spigot is not supported anymore.

## New

### Enchantment
- **Levels:** WoodCutter I, II and III – higher levels cut bigger trees (120 / 180 / 240 logs by default). The
  enchanting table gives level I (60 %), II (30 %) or III (10 %).
- **Anvil:** two items of the same level are combined to the next level, a book puts the enchantment onto an axe.
- **Grindstone:** removes the enchantment.
- **Books:** books can get the enchantment at the enchanting table as well.
- **Goodie:** `goodie: permission` or `goodie: everyone` lets players cut trees with every axe, without the
  enchantment.

### Cutting trees
- **Every tree:** all logs of the game are supported, including cherry, mangrove and the huge fungi of the Nether.
  Branches with gaps (e.g. acacias) are found as well.
- **Falling animation:** the tree falls log by log from the bottom to the top (`animation`), or at once as before.
- **Fast leaf decay:** the leaves of a cut tree decay within seconds (`fast-leaf-decay`).
- **Replanting:** a new sapling is planted where the tree stood, for free or from the inventory of the player
  (`replant`).
- **Drops:** logs can be put straight into the inventory (`drops-to-inventory`) and drop a second time with a
  chance (`bonus-drops`).
- **Costs:** cutting a tree can cost food points or experience levels (`costs`).
- **Other plugins:** every log is broken as the player, so protection, logging and skill plugins handle each log.
### Commands and permissions
- `/woodcutter toggle` – players switch WoodCutter on or off for themselves.
- `/woodcutter give <player> [book|axe] [level]` – gives an enchanted book or axe, also from the console
  (e.g. as a reward).
- `/woodcutter enchant [level]` – now with a level.
- Tab completion for all commands.
- New permissions: `woodcutter.use` (default: everyone), `woodcutter.goodie`, `woodcutter.bypass.delay` and
  `woodcutter.maxblocks.<amount>` for a higher limit of logs, e.g. for ranks.

### Server
- **Folia** is supported, with the same jar.
- **messages.yml:** all texts of the plugin can be changed now.
- **Disabled worlds** accept patterns: `sg_*` matches `sg_1`, `sg_lobby`, …
- The config is updated automatically: new settings are added with their comments, your values stay.
