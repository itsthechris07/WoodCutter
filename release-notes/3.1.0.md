# WoodCutter 3.1.0

## New

- **WorldGuard** (optional): the region flag `woodcutter` – `/rg flag <region> woodcutter deny` disables WoodCutter
  in a region.
- **PlaceholderAPI** (optional): `%woodcutter_enabled%`, `%woodcutter_level%`, `%woodcutter_max_blocks%` and
  `%woodcutter_delay%`.

## Changed

- **Falling animation:** every layer of the tree falls from the trunk to the tips of its branches, and the sound gets
  higher towards the top.
- **Fast leaf decay:** with the animation the leaves follow the logs from the bottom to the top instead of
  disappearing at once.
- **Config update:** files without missing settings are not rewritten anymore, so own comments stay. Broken files
  are never overwritten.

## Fixed

- Texts that are missing in the `messages.yml` have the default text instead of being empty.
